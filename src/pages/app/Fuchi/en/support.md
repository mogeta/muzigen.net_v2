## Contact

[muzigen@gmail.com](mailto:muzigen@gmail.com?subject=Fuchi%20support)

## Information to Include

For bug reports or purchase-related inquiries, please include the following information where possible:

- Device model
- OS type and version
- Fuchi app version
- Date and time when the issue occurred
- Steps taken before the issue occurred
- Error messages or screenshots, if available
- For purchase or billing inquiries, order number and purchase date

## Common Checks

### If the app does not work correctly

- Check whether the app is up to date.
- Check whether your device OS is supported.
- Try restarting your device or switching network environments.

### Purchase and billing issues

- Purchase status may take some time to be reflected.
- Check your purchase history in the relevant app store.
- Refunds and cancellations may be processed according to the rules of each app store or payment service.

## Replies

We will review your inquiry and reply as necessary. Depending on the content, investigation may take time, or we may not be able to reply individually.
