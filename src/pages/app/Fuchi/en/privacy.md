## 1. Basic Policy

muzigen.net ("we," "us," or "our") handles user information in the application "Fuchi" ("the app") appropriately in accordance with applicable laws and this Privacy Policy.

The app is designed based on the policy that it **does not collect or externally transmit any personal information from users**.

## 2. Information Collected

The app does not collect or externally transmit any information, including personal information.

- **Photos and images**: Image processing is completed on the user's device, and images are not transmitted outside the device.
- **Device information, OS version, operation logs, crash logs, etc.**: Not collected.
- **Advertising identifiers, cookies, and similar technologies**: Not used.

The following information may be stored **only on the user's device** for app functionality. This information is not transmitted outside the device.

- In-app purchase status
- Free-use count and settings

## 3. Purpose of Use

As stated above, we do not collect user information and therefore do not use it. Information stored only on the user's device is used solely for the following purposes:

- To provide paid features and manage purchase status
- To provide free-use count limits
- To retain user settings on the device

## 4. Use of External Services

The app does not use external services for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or similar purposes, and no data is transmitted by such SDKs.

In-app purchases may be processed through the Apple App Store (Apple Inc.) or Google Play (Google LLC). Payment information, such as credit card information, is managed by each platform provider and is not obtained by us. Please refer to each provider's privacy policy for how they handle information.

## 5. Third-Party Disclosure

Because we do not collect users' personal information, we do not provide such information to third parties.

## 6. Security Management

Images and settings handled by the app are managed on the user's device. By design, the app does not have a data transmission path to external servers, which minimizes the risk of information leakage.

## 7. Disclosure, Correction, Suspension of Use, etc.

Because we do not retain users' personal information, there is no information subject to disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, or suspension of use. If you have any questions, please contact us through the support contact.

## 8. Use by Minors

If a minor uses the app, including in-app purchases, the minor must do so with the consent of a parent or guardian.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may change this Privacy Policy as necessary. Important changes will be announced on this page or within the app.

## 10. Contact

For inquiries about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the [Support page](/app/Fuchi/en/support).
